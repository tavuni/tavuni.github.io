Support

Need help? Email tavuni.support@gmail.com and we'll reply within 2 business days.

How do I change my buddy?

Open the My Buddy tab, swipe to the buddy you want and tap Choose.

How do I plan my week and get reminders?

In My Buddy, scroll to Your week, tap the days you want and turn on Remind me. If reminders don't arrive, check Settings > Notifications > TAVUNI on your iPhone.

How does training with my buddy work?

Your buddy trains at the same time as you, and the app keeps time for each set and rest. Pause or add rest anytime.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. TAVUNI has no accounts and doesn't send your workouts anywhere. Deleting the app deletes your data.

How do I see the intro again?

Settings > Help > How TAVUNI works.

Is TAVUNI medical advice?

No. TAVUNI is a workout companion. Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise program, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.

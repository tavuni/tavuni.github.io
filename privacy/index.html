Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

TAVUNI ("we") makes the TAVUNI workout app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What we collect

Only anonymous usage events, described under Analytics below. TAVUNI has no accounts and never collects your name, email address, contacts, location, health details or anything you type.

What stays on your phone

To work, the app saves your choices and activity on your device: your first name if you add one, the buddy you picked, your workout style, home or gym, your weekly plan and reminder times, the workouts you record, and your appearance settings. This information stays in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see it.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device. They are not sent through our servers.

Analytics

To learn which features help people keep training, the app sends anonymous usage events to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in the EU. Examples: the app was opened, a workout was started or finished (with the exercise, number of sets and length), reminders were turned on, or the appearance setting changed, along with your iPhone's system version.

Each install of the app gets a random ID that is hashed on your phone before anything is sent, and TelemetryDeck hashes it again. These events cannot be linked to your name, your Apple ID or other apps.

Ads and tracking

TAVUNI has no ads, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell or share data with data brokers.

Deleting your information

Deleting the app removes everything it saved on your device and resets the random ID.

Children

TAVUNI is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If a future version collects information, for example for accounts or subscriptions, we will update this policy before that version is released and show the change in the app.

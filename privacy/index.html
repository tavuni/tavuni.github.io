Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

TAVUNI ("we", "us") makes the TAVUNI workout app for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app and this website. Questions: tavuni.support@gmail.com.

The short version

No account. No ads. No tracking across apps. Your workouts stay on your phone. The app sends only anonymous usage events, and you can turn those off in Settings.

What stays on your phone

To work, the app saves your choices and activity on your device: your first name if you add one, the buddy you picked, your workout style, home or gym, your weekly plan and reminder times, the workouts you record, and your appearance settings. This information stays in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see it.

Anonymous usage data

To learn which features help people keep training, the app sends anonymous usage events to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. Examples: the app was opened, a workout was started or finished (with the exercise, number of sets and length), reminders were turned on, the appearance setting changed, or the answers you tap in the optional feedback questions, along with your iPhone's system version.

Each install of the app gets a random ID that is hashed on your phone before anything is sent, and TelemetryDeck hashes it again. These events cannot be linked to your name, your Apple ID or other apps. We use them only to understand and improve TAVUNI.

You can turn this off anytime in Settings > Privacy > Share anonymous usage data. When it is off, the app sends nothing.

Feedback and email

If you type a note in the feedback questions, it is sent only if you choose to send it by email from your own mail app. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. We delete support emails when they are no longer needed.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device. They are not sent through our servers.

Who receives data

TelemetryDeck processes the anonymous usage events for us and may store them on servers outside your country, including in the European Union. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use data for advertising.

How long data is kept

Information on your phone stays until you delete the app. Anonymous usage events are kept by TelemetryDeck for up to the retention period of our plan (currently 3 months) and then deleted.

Ads, tracking and Do Not Track

TAVUNI has no ads and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Because we do not track you, there is nothing for a browser "Do Not Track" signal to turn off. We add no cookies or analytics to this website; GitHub, which hosts it, may keep standard server logs.

Your choices

Turn off anonymous usage data in Settings. Change or remove your name in Settings > Your name. Deleting the app removes everything it saved on your device and resets the random ID. Because usage events cannot be linked to you, we cannot look them up or delete them for a specific person, but you can ask us anything at tavuni.support@gmail.com.

Children

TAVUNI is meant for people 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

The app sends usage events over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Information on your phone is protected by your iPhone's security, such as your passcode.

Changes

If a future version collects more information, for example for accounts or subscriptions, we will update this policy before that version is released, change the effective date above, and show the change in the app.

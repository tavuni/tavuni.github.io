Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

TAVUNI ("we") makes the TAVUNI workout app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What we collect

Nothing. TAVUNI has no accounts and does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

To work, the app saves your choices and activity on your device: the buddy you picked, your workout style, home or gym, your weekly plan and reminder times, the workouts you record, and your appearance settings. This information stays in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see it.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device. They are not sent through our servers.

Analytics and ads

TAVUNI does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

Deleting your information

Deleting the app removes everything it saved on your device.

Children

TAVUNI is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If a future version collects information, for example for accounts or subscriptions, we will update this policy before that version is released and show the change in the app.
